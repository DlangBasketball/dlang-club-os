# DLang Sports OS: try it from your team's perspective

**Open:** `https://dlang-staging.vercel.app/demo` (staging; to be replaced by the public demo address before external release)

You get your own fictional club, created on your first click. Nothing you do reaches a real person: no e-mail, SMS or push notification is sent, and payments are sandbox records. Your club is separate from every other visitor's. It stays available for 24 hours.

## The two-minute path

1. **Start as Jamie** (parent) → Club programmes → U14 Falcons → choose **Alex** → **Send request**.
2. **Start as Club Admin** → Approvals → Alex Morgan · U14 Falcons → **Confirm**.
3. **Start as Coach Taylor** → My team → **Alex Morgan is on the U14 Falcons roster.** Same athlete, nobody typed anything twice.

## Each role: your first click

| Role | Button on /demo | You land on | Try this |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parent | Start as Jamie | Parent home | Switch between Alex and Sam with the athlete chips. Check each child's schedule and status. Request U14 Falcons for Alex. Book the Skills Camp for Sam. |
| Club Admin | Start as Club Admin | Approvals | Confirm Alex for U14 Falcons, then open Teams & rosters → U14 Falcons. |
| Coach | Start as Coach Taylor | My team | Find Alex on the roster. Open Today's trainings → today's practice → mark Alex **Present** → **Mark all present** → **Review and finalize**. You only see your own team. |
| Athlete | Start as Alex | Player home | See the schedule, then Attendance: today's practice shows **Present** once the coach has finalized. |
| Finance | Start as Finance | Invoices | Open Jamie's booking invoice and its payment status. Casey Brooks's camp booking is a second example, already paid by sandbox card with the same reference on invoice and payment. |

Switch roles at any time with the **Demo · Switch role** button (bottom left). Your changes carry through to every role.

## Start over

On `/demo`, press **Reset demo**. Your club goes back to the starting scenario (Alex not registered, Sam on U12 Comets). Other visitors are not affected.
