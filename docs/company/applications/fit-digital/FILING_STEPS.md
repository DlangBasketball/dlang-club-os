## 3. Eligibility questions to ask Innovaud first

1. **Company age.** If FIT Digital requires register entry within the last 6 months (Grant) or 12 months (Seed), does Swiss Preparatory Sports Academy GmbH qualify, given its register entry date [Confirm] and any earlier academy activity? If not, should the software project sit in a new GmbH (and what does that mean for the IP assignment and the investor's convertible)?
2. **Team.** Does a founder plus a fractional CTO (and AI-assisted engineering under human review) meet the "team of 2 to 5, at least one domiciled in Vaud" condition? Does the CTO need to be engaged (decision 8 Jan 2027) before filing?
3. **Beta.** Does DLang (the founder's own academy) count as a test customer, or is at least one external test club required?
4. **Timing.** Next committee dates and their submission cut-offs; current application form and annex list.
5. **Eligible costs.** Are external services (penetration test, lawyer, translation, contractor engineering, hosting) eligible? Founder salary excluded?
6. **Sequencing.** If the Grant is awarded, when can the Seed loan be requested, and does the 12-month clock run from register entry?
