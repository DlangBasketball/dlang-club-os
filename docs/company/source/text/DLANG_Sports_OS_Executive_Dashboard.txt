Basis of preparation. This dashboard recomputes the base, bull and bear
cases from the same assumptions as DLANG_Sports_OS_Financial_Model.xlsx.
Market figures are sourced in Appendix A of the business plan. DLANG
Sports OS has zero paying external clubs as at 18 August 2026 — every
growth, churn and revenue figure here is a modelled assumption, not a
measured outcome. Figures supplied by the founder (athlete counts, warm
network size, available founder funds, existing build state, round
terms) have not been independently verified.
Key assumptions. Blended ARPA CHF 330 / 400 / 450 by year. Logo churn
1.5% a month in the base case. Support delivery CHF 55 per club per
month — the line that sets gross margin. Payments: 50% of collections
through the platform at a 0.9% net margin, QR-bill free. Onboarding fee
CHF 900 average, waived for the five founding clubs. EUR/CHF 0.93. FIT
Digital Grant CHF 20,000 modelled at 100% probability — set it to 50%
for the risk-adjusted view.
