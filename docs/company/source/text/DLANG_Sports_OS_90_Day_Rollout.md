**00 HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT**

This is not a strategy document. It is a schedule. It assumes the
decisions in the business plan have been taken and answers a narrower
question: what happens on Monday.

Five rules govern the whole ninety days.

1.  **One thing ships every week.** However small. A week with no
    shipped change is a week the founding clubs stop believing the
    roadmap.

2.  **One selling conversation happens every week, minimum.** Building
    without selling is how a founder spends a year producing something
    nobody buys. The discovery calls in weeks 1 to 4 are the most
    important work in this document.

3.  **The gates are real.** Three gates, at weeks 4, 8 and 13. Each has
    a written consequence for failure. The consequence is decided now,
    in calm conditions, precisely so it can be followed later in
    stressed ones.

4.  **Compliance is not phase two.** No real club data touches the
    system before the record of processing, the data protection impact
    assessment and the data processing agreement exist. Children\'s and
    health data with personal liability is not a corner to cut.

5.  **Write the weekly note.** Every Friday, one paragraph: what
    shipped, what sold, what is blocked, what is the number. Even with
    no one to send it to. It takes ten minutes and it is the only
    reliable defence against a founder losing objectivity about their
    own company.
