## Part B: COPPA direct notice and verifiable parental consent (US, child under 13)
_Required by 16 CFR §312.4(c) and §312.5, as amended in 2025 (compliance date
22 April 2026)._

**B1. What we collect from or about your child:** name, date of birth, team and
programme, attendance, and the health and emergency information you provide. We
do **not** let your child create a public profile, chat publicly or upload content.

**B2. Why:** only to register your child and run the programme safely.

**B3. Who we disclose it to:** our processors (listed at [SUBPROCESSOR LINK]) under
contract. We disclose data to other third parties (for example a league or
federation for player registration) **only with your separate consent (B5)**.
Refusing B5 will not stop your child from taking part.

**B4. Retention:** we keep your child's information only as long as reasonably
necessary (see our written retention policy [LINK]) and then delete it.

**B5. Optional disclosure to third parties:** [ ] I consent to the club sharing my
child's name, date of birth and team with [LEAGUE / FEDERATION NAME] for player
registration.

**B6. Your rights:** you may review the personal information collected about your
child, ask us to delete it, and refuse to let us collect or use it further, at any
time. Contact [EMAIL / PHONE / ADDRESS].

**B7. How we verify that you are the parent** (the club chooses one or more):
- [ ] A small **payment card transaction** in your name, as part of registration (Stripe). Payment reference recorded: [REF]
- [ ] A **signed form** returned by post, scan or e-signature
- [ ] A **video call** with trained club staff
- [ ] A **government ID check**, with the image deleted immediately after verification (we keep a reference, never the ID)
- [ ] **Knowledge-based authentication**
- [ ] The **school** authorises collection (school context only; see the Student-Data Addendum)

> If consent is not obtained within a reasonable time (the club's policy: **14 days**),
> we delete the parent's contact details collected for the purpose of asking.
