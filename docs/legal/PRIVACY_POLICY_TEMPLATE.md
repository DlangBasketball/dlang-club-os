## 5. Children
- A parent or guardian registers each child and exercises the child's rights.
- **EU:** for online services offered directly to a child, we rely on parental
  consent below the national digital age of consent (13–16 depending on the
  member state; GDPR Art. 8).
- **Switzerland:** a child who has capacity of judgement may exercise their own
  rights. We still ask a parent to consent for under-16s.
- **US (COPPA):** for children **under 13** we give parents direct notice and
  obtain **verifiable parental consent** before we collect personal information.
  Parents may review their child's data, have it deleted, and refuse further
  collection. Consent to share data with third parties is requested
  **separately** and is never required in order to take part. We keep children's
  data only as long as reasonably necessary (see section 7) and protect it under a
  written information-security programme.

## 8. Your rights
You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to
processing of your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does
not affect processing that took place before it. Signed-in parents can download
their data and request deletion from **Account → Privacy**. You can also write
to [EMAIL].
We reply within **30 days** (CH/EU) or **45 days** (US state laws). We may need to
verify your identity first.

- **California and other US states with privacy laws, where applicable:** right to
  know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither), and to
  limit the use of sensitive personal information. You may use an authorised
  agent. We honour **Global Privacy Control**. We will not discriminate against you
  for exercising these rights.
- **Schools (FERPA):** see the Student-Data Addendum. Parents and eligible
  students exercise their inspection and amendment rights through the school.

**Complaints:** CH: FDPIC (www.edoeb.admin.ch). EU: your local supervisory
authority. UK: ICO. US: FTC or your state Attorney General.

## 9. Security
Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest; database row-level security per
club and role; immutable consent records; audit logging of sensitive changes;
least-privilege staff access; backups; and a breach-response procedure that
notifies authorities within **72 hours** where required.
