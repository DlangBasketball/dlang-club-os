                                              OVERVIEW
\d The
   facilitates
       DLang OSmanagement.
                       App (DLang OS) will be created to meet a need for technology in management of
    sports teams and clubs. Most of the systems in the market today have a language and formatting
    geared mainly towards the FITNESS world. The great majority of these products present diﬃculty
    adjusting to the wide range of segments within sports activities management. DLang OS solves these
    problems and make management easy.
    DLang OS works with a modern, easy-to-use platform, that makes the insertion, removal, and editing of
    information simple to the user. Development and managing of daily tasks such as creation of classes,
    roll calls, clock-in for employees, student evaluations, signup and sign in for classes and
    events,ﬁnancial reports , main calendar, rank of students with a student emoji showing his progress,
    are some of the features. All with a dynamic standalone version dashboard for manager, employees
    and clients.
    It separates itself from other products in the market because it creates a constant interaction
    between managers and employees, and employees and clients. It is focused in the creation of better
    quality of management for employees, students, and parents, as well as managers, with
    customization of the tools to serve the client in its most varied situations.
